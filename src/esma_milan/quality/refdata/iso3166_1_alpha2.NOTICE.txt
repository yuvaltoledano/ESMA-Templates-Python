iso3166_1_alpha2.txt holds the 249 two-letter country codes of ISO 3166-1,
one per line, and nothing else (no names, no translations).

Source:  the Debian "iso-codes" package, version 4.20.1,
         file data/iso_3166-1.json, taken from the source archive
